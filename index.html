Spreads Knowledge w/o Being Used Up

Knowledge Spillover

Ideas created by one firm or person can raise productivity for others.

Unlike a machine, knowledge is non-rival: using it does not use it up. It spreads through workers, networks, suppliers, universities, and nearby firms.

Sources: Nature Index, NBER

Spreads Knowledge w/o Being Used Up

  • Close distance helps ideas move quickly.
  • Job switching carries tacit knowledge.
  • Informal networks turn local talk into innovation.
  • Fast diffusion raises the whole cluster's productivity.
  • Talent and capital make the cycle self-reinforcing.
